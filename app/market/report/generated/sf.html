Most of the market. What they can pay is set by the mortgage rate, and the mortgage rate is set by the bond market.

Home Loans Follow the Bond

Gold is your home loan, blue is what the government pays. The gap between them barely moves, so the bond leads and your rate follows.

The Cash Buyer

4.8% of the sales, 19% of the money. They pay cash, so the mortgage rate is beside the point.

$5–10M Sales by Month — 2025 vs 2026

146 sales against 71 last year, +106%. Watch the shape: 28 in June, 11 in July when the stock market fell 10%, back up in August after OpenAI let staff cash out $7 billion of shares. Last year had no summer dip — so this is the stock market, not the season.

What an $8M House Costs to Just Hold

A cash buyer pays no interest — they give up interest. That same $8M in government bonds earns $402K a year; add property tax and holding the house runs $496K a year.
What moves them. The financed buyer moves when the ten-year Treasury moves. Under 4.5% home loans fall into the low 6s and buyers come back out; hold at 5% and loans head toward 6.82% and fewer people qualify. Watch the bond, not the Fed.
What moves them. The cash buyer moves on two things: what their money earns sitting still — 5.02% right now, tax-free of California — and whether they can get at it, which means share sales and IPOs. One thing outranks both: need. A family that has outgrown the house buys this year at whatever the market asks.
Payments are principal and interest only. Foregone yield uses the 10-year Treasury; property tax at 1.18%. Insurance, upkeep and illiquidity are additional.page 4 / 9

How a Mortgage Rate Actually Gets Set

  • 5.02 — % — what the government pays to borrow for ten years
  • 1.74 — Points the bank adds on top for its costs and its risk
  • 6.76 — % — your home loan rate. Those two, added together
  • 0.98 — Points the government's rate rose in twelve months

The Yield Curve Today

What you earn lending the government money for three months, two years, ten years and thirty. Normally the line rises — lend longer, earn more — and it does today. When it tips the other way, short money paying more than long, it is called inverted, and that has come before every U.S. recession since the 1970s. We are not there.
Rents. A one-bedroom now runs $4,250 a month, +25% more than last year — the fastest rise in the country. That same $4,250 covers the payment on a $655K mortgage, which is why renters keep turning into buyers.

What Pulls a Cash Buyer Back

  • Stocks — falling. Their shares are the down payment, and the market dropped twice this year
  • 5.02 — % — what cash earns doing nothing. The higher that goes, the better waiting looks
  • No way — to sell shares. Rich on paper is not the same as able to buy

What Brings Them In

  • Need — first. A growing family, a move, a school year — none of it waits for the Nasdaq
  • $7B — OpenAI tender in August at an $852B valuation. 300+ new decamillionaires
  • Scarcity — 41 trophy sales a year in Pacific Heights. You buy when one appears
  • Prop 13 — Buy now and the assessment is locked at today's price for as long as you hold
